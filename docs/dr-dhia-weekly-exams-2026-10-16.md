# Dr Dhia weekly cyber-governance papers

The six supplied PDFs were transcribed into `weeklyCyberExams.json`. Five
weekly papers retain their original order and alternatives: weeks 1, 3, 4, 5
and 6 contain 15, 10, 10, 20 and 15 MCQs. The image on page 2 of Open Book Quiz
was visually read and retained as a separate CISO/ISO 27001 scenario. No week 2
paper was supplied. Name, email and Google Forms interface fields are excluded.

Question provenance includes each original document SHA-256 and page. Arabic
spelling and punctuation were reviewed; question meaning and option order are
preserved. The sources contain questions rather than an original marking key.
The educational solutions are labelled accordingly. The open scenario permits
assumptions; its model explicitly labels its fictional institution and follows
the existing ISO roadmap. Its 20-point rubric is proposed and receives no
automatic essay score. MCQs provide checked feedback after a submitted choice.

The collection appears as **اختبارات د. ضياء الأسبوعية** within cyber
governance's exam-material section. Practice, question-paper and model-answer
views share the same server-owned bank. PDF exports use the existing Arabic
font/RTL renderer. Initial question DTOs contain no answer fields; feedback and
model answers are requested explicitly through authenticated learner routes.
These are revision papers, separate from timed challenge attempts and their
protected answer-release policy. No trainee identities or drafts are stored.

All routes require an active learner or guest identity and a published subject;
the collection is unavailable from other courses. Input schemas, export rate
limits, private/no-store responses and an allowlisted web proxy protect the
new surface. No upload endpoint or admin permission bypass is added.
